How we calculate this

We build the number flow by flow from your revenue, order value and list size. Every rate below is on the conservative side of what well-run Klaviyo stores see.

  • Visitors: worked back from your orders at a 1.8% store conversion rate.
  • Welcome series: an optimised pop-up captures 2.5% of visitors, and 3% of new subscribers buy during the series.
  • Abandoned checkout: about 1.2 checkouts are abandoned per order, 75% leave an email, and the flow wins back 6% of them.
  • Browse abandonment: 2% of visits are known subscribers viewing products, and 1.5% of those come back and buy.
  • Post-purchase: 3% of customers place an extra order they would not have placed otherwise.
  • Winback: brings lapsed customers back, worth about 1.5% of monthly orders.
  • Campaigns: each send to your engaged list earns about 0.12% of your order value per subscriber, with a haircut for every extra send per week so more is never free.
  • Your email today: "Nothing" assumes Shopify's default abandoned cart email. "Basic flows" assumes 45% of what a full system earns. "Flows + campaigns" assumes 70%, plus one untargeted send a week. We only count the difference.
  • Timing: month one runs at half speed while the system goes live. Your list grows each month from the better pop-up, less 1.5% churn.

Your results depend on your product, your traffic and your offer. That's why the first thing we do is a revenue audit of your actual Klaviyo account.
